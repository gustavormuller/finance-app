## Deviations and decisions

All recorded with reasons in `docs/DEFERRED.md` (005 CP1–CP3). The ones that shape later work:

- **Income/expense aggregations sum BRL rows only**; `total` is BRL-only per spec, foreign
  accounts carry `excludedFromTotal: true`. Foreign-currency activity is invisible in month,
  monthly and by-category until conversion exists.
- **Signs as stored**: `expense` and Expense amounts are negative; `net = income + expense`;
  `share` is positive, largest-remainder rounded to sum to exactly 1.0000.
- **`months` clamped to [1, 36]**; malformed parameters are 400s naming the field; `kind=Transfer`
  is a 400 with its own message.
- **API "current month" is UTC**; the web always sends `month` computed from the local date.
- **Month selector** drives both the month totals and the category breakdown; kept in state,
  not the URL.
- **Category breakdown is HTML bars, not Recharts**; the 12-month chart is Recharts with a
  visually hidden table. Blue/orange chart colours (green/red failed the colour-vision check).
- **Empty state** only when there are no accounts and every month is zero; an account with no
  transactions shows the dashboard.
- **Existing users** create Transferência by hand on the categories page (no backfill).
- **E2E 26** creates Transfer rows directly (Saída and Entrada) rather than recategorising an
  expense, since recategorising an expense would rightly change the month's expense. New users
  already have Transferência seeded, so the test does not create one.

## Known debts

- **English problem-details title** still shows on the transactions and categories pages: they
  render `ApiError.message` (`detail ?? title`), and a 400 has no `detail`. Fixed on the account
  form only (CP3).
- **`/monthly` ends at the UTC month** and takes no `month` parameter; the web asks for 13 months
  and keeps the 12 up to the local month. A browser east of UTC sees the series end a month
  early at the turn of a month.

  `api/Program.cs`, `api/Domain/IUserOwned.cs`, `api/Domain/Transactions/Category.cs`,

## Lessons

- **inotify, not flakiness.** Test hosts watching config files exhausted
  `fs.inotify.max_user_instances`; the failures looked random until the suite grew (4c5da41).
- **Two kinds baked into the web.** The sign-from-kind assumption lived in three components;
  a `Record<CategoryKind, …>` in `labels.ts` made the third kind a type error until handled.
- **Chart legends follow render order.** The Recharts legend and tooltip listed series in the
  wrong order and in series colour until fixed (be7f117).
