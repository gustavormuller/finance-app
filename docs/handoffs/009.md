## Documentation flags (not edited)

- **ADR-003's wording is stretched by the sweep.** The ADR (final form, CP1) says "on
  startup, any `Pending` row older than 5 minutes is re-enqueued". **The sweep runs at
  startup and then every 5 minutes** (CP5a). Otherwise a restart within 5 minutes of a POST
  leaves that row `Pending`, with the card spinning, until the next boot. The sweep also
  **fails** a `Running` row past its timeout plus 5 minutes instead of re-running it,
  because its call may already have been paid for. The ADR and spec say nothing about
  `Running` rows. Either amend the ADR's sentence to "at startup and every 5 minutes" and
  mention `Running`, or say to cut the sweep back to startup only.
- **ARCHITECTURE.md still describes AI that 009 does not build** (CP1). These are not ADRs,

## Deviations and decisions

All are recorded with reasons in `docs/DEFERRED.md` (009 · CP1 to CP7). Items marked
**(spec silent)** are guesses that need your answer.

- **Decided by you:** prices are in USD, converted at the latest USDBRL or `Ai:UsdBrl`
  (CP2; the spec's key names got a one-line note, 2d85ecc). The analysis sees 007's summary
  only, not 008's returns (CP2).
- **Gates and money:**
  - The month is read on a fixed UTC-3 clock, for usage and the budget (CP2).
  - A failed call that reports no tokens is estimated at 4 characters a token. It errs
    towards refusing. A 5xx is billed at that estimate unless the adapter says 0 (CP2, CP3).
  - The budget cut-off comes before each call, so a month can end over by one call (CP2).
  - `AiGateway` checks `ai_enabled` as well as the endpoints. It also stops a job whose user
    turned AI off after the POST (CP2).
  - No rate limit on suggest or on generate. The budget is the bound (CP4b, CP5b).
- **Providers (spec silent):**
  - Typed `HttpClient`s, no SDK. One send and no retries (CP3).
  - A truncated answer (`max_tokens`, `length`) or a refusal is a failure that carries its
    tokens, not partial text (CP3).
  - Timeouts: categorisation 30 s (decision 4), analysis **120 s, my choice** (CP3).
  - A 4xx is billed 0. A 5xx or a dropped connection is estimated (CP3).
- **Categorisation (spec silent):**
  - Normalized descriptions and each row's kind are sent. No amounts. Real GUIDs as ids.
    Short aliases would cut output tokens about three times (CP4a).
  - Batches of 40, in sequence, in one request. 200 rows can take up to 5 × 30 s. Check
    Caddy's and the browser's timeouts in manual step 3 (CP4a).
  - A Transfer category is kept on either sign, through rule 3 (CP4a).
  - `suggested` counts changed rows only. A row the model answers with its current default
    stays `Default` (CP4a).
  - The system prompt is an English constant, not a prompt file (CP4a).
  - The "Outros" category picked by hand is `User` and never sent. Batches staged before
    009 read `None` and are never sent: re-upload them (CP1, CP4b).
  - A 502 for provider failures other than a timeout (CP4b).
- **Analysis (spec silent):**
  - The input's shape (CP5a). Balances and the portfolio are **current, not as of the
    analysed month's end**.
  - Merchant names are normalized expense descriptions. **A PIX to a person names them.**
    Income descriptions are never sent (CP5a).
  - `MaxTokens` 8000. One consumer, so analyses run one at a time (CP5a).
  - The sweep, above (CP5a).
  - A 409 while the month is `Pending` or `Running`. Regenerate resets the row in place,
    and **the old content is cleared at once**, not when the new one lands (CP5b).
  - The current month may be analysed. A future month is a 400 (CP5b).
  - `GET /api/ai/usage` answers with AI off, and carries `month` (CP5b).
- **Web (spec silent):**
  - The card polls the month's list read, not the id (CP6).
  - With AI off, the buttons stay visible, disabled, with the reason, which names
    Configurações with no link (CP6).
  - "Regenerar" on `Failed` too, with no confirmation (CP6).
  - The provider is named "a Anthropic (Claude) ou a OpenAI (ChatGPT)", because the API does
    not expose `Ai:Provider` (CP6).
  - The spend on `/settings` is the UTC-3 month, rounded to the cent (CP6).

## A UI bug found by E2E 28 (CP7)

It was fixed with a failing unit test first.

- **The AI switch went back to off for one tick after a click.** CP6 made the switch show
  the state it was saving while the PATCH was in flight. It read that state from the
  mutation, which reaches the page on the query client's next tick. In that tick the
  controlled checkbox was re-rendered unchecked. Playwright's `check()` failed on the first
  run ("Clicking the checkbox did not change its state"), and a person could see a flicker.
  The switch now keeps the state it is saving in its own state, set in the click handler
  (0b50936). Test: `SettingsPage.test.tsx`, a `fireEvent.click` asserted synchronously.

## Lessons

- **Playwright catches what a unit test's `await` hides.** CP6's switch test used
  `userEvent`, whose awaits let the mutation's tick pass, so it was green while the real
  switch flickered. A synchronous assertion right after `fireEvent.click` shows it.
- **Make a date-shaped E2E read the browser's clock, then run it on another date.** Test 29
  pages back from the browser's own month to the fixture's. A throwaway copy with the
  browser clock fixed in March 2027 proved it, instead of trusting that today is September.
- **A fake that echoes its input makes E2E assertions exact.** The analysis fake quotes the
  month and its expense from what was sent, so test 29 checks the whole input path, not just
  that some text appeared.
